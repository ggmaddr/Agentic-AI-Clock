# Banoffee Pie

**Servings:** 8-10  
**Prep:** 30 mins  
**Cook:** 35 mins  

## Ingredients

### For the pastry
- 100g butter, chilled (plus extra for greasing)
- 200g plain flour
- 1 medium egg, separated
- 1 tbsp golden caster sugar

### For the filling
- 4 sliced bananas
- 394g caramel (or dulce de leche)
- 300ml double cream (optional)
- Dark chocolate (optional, for garnish)

## Method

1. **Make the pastry case**  
   Put butter and flour in a food processor, pulse until it resembles fresh breadcrumbs. Add the yolk of the egg and the sugar, pulse again until mixed through, then add cold water a tablespoon at a time, pulsing after each addition. Stop adding water as soon as the dough starts to come together as one piece. Form a smooth ball, wrap in cling film, and chill in the fridge for 30 minutes.

2. **Pre-bake the pastry**  
   Heat the oven to 190 C (or 170 C fan)/gas 4. Grease a 23 cm loose‑bottomed fluted tart tin. Roll out the chilled pastry on a lightly floured surface and line the tin, leaving about ½ cm of pastry above the edge. Trim the excess with kitchen scissors.  
   Line the base with a piece of baking parchment and weigh it down with baking beans or uncooked rice. Bake for 15 minutes, then remove the paper and beans and brush the pastry surface with beaten egg white. Return to the oven for a further 15–20 minutes, or until crisp, golden and cooked through. Cool completely (this can be done a day ahead).

3. **Add the filling**  
   Spread half of the caramel over the cooled pastry case and layer the sliced bananas on top. Cover with the remaining caramel and chill.

4. **Top and serve**  
   Whip the cream until thick and spoon it over the top of the tart. Finely grate 1–2 pieces of dark chocolate over the whipped cream to garnish. Serve immediately.

> **Tip:** The pastry case can be made a day ahead and kept chilled; add the whipped cream just before serving.
